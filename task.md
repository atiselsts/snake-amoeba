Make another game, this one about a snake that looks slightly like amoeba.
It eats food (and other snakes).

The snakes size is shown as number on top of its head.

The food is on the ground and elsewhere where a snake can access it. There are at least 2 types of food: smaller and bigger round bits.

There are multiple levels and types of maps (plain ; forest ; cactus land).

If two snakes meet the bigger one eats the smaller one.

If a snake does not eat anything its size goes down slowly (a constant process over time).

The player when starting a level can choose how the snake looks: e.g. with hearts, stripes, plain etc.
